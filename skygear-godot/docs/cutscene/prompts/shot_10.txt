Use ref_image_0 for the exact palette and painting style: moonlight cool and
silver from the upper left, ember-orange glow from the lower right, violet
storm-dusk behind. Use ref_image_1 and ref_image_2 for the exact character —
the same young man in every detail: a red brass-buckled greatcoat with a high

gauntlet. Same face, same hair, same eyes, no drifting features.

The video: a portrait. The young sky-corsair captain, head and shoulders only,
filling the frame, turned very slightly three-quarters toward the camera and
looking straight down the lens. Behind him nothing but deep violet storm shadow
falling to near-black at the edges — no deck, no rigging, no horizon, no
objects. Warm ember lantern light from the lower right catches the brass of his
goggles and his collar; cool moonlight rims his hair and one shoulder. He
breathes, his hair and collar stir faintly in the wind, and the camera pushes
in very slowly and steadily. His expression holds throughout: calm, level,
faintly defiant. He does not smile, does not speak, does not look away.

A character portrait — still, centred, symmetrical lighting, painted like a
plate in an illustrated book. No text, no watermark, no border, no frame, no
photorealism, no motion blur, no camera shake.

Proportions are locked to the character references: stylized chibi build — a large expressive head, compact heroic body roughly three and a half heads tall, small hands and feet, oversized weapon. Keep the references' painterly brushwork, fine brass detailing and warm rim-light at that scale. Do NOT restyle anyone to realistic adult proportions.

World rule, absolute: this is a SKY world and the ship is an AIRSHIP miles above the ground. There is NO sea, NO ocean, NO water, NO waves, NO shoreline and NO land anywhere in frame. Below and beyond the ship there is only open air, cloud and storm; any horizon is a bank of cloud, never a waterline.
