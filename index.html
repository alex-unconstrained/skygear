v11 — the current build

v11 · The deck fights back

The last build was won by standing at maximum range and healing faster than three lanes of boarders could hurt you. This one pays you for closing instead: fighting inside your own reach builds pressure, and a full gauge vents — scalding steam, everything thrown off you, and you close up.

The kegs stacked on the deck are live now. So are the crates and the lanterns. And there is more deck to fight over, two dashes to cross it with, and enemy fire you can actually see coming.

Play v11

What is new

  • Pressure & ventLand hits inside your own reach and the gauge under your health fills. Full, it vents: 40 damage in a ring, everything knocked off you, and 15 health back.
  • Live ordnanceShoot a steam keg and it lights a fuse, shakes, and detonates — on boarders, on you, and on the next keg. Crates spill salvage. Lanterns spill fire.
  • Healing that costs somethingBloodsteam only heals from damage dealt up close, and no amount of stacking can out-heal the deck any more.
  • More deck, more dashA wider deck, thinner cargo runs, a third cross-passage, two dash charges from the start, and a dash that hurts what it goes through.
  • Fire you can readEnemy bolts are hostile-coloured, slower, trailed, shadowed on the deck — and a shooter now draws its firing line before it fires.
  • More soundA boss theme and a track for the last three waves, plus the deck's own voices: fuses, blasts, breaking crates, the vent.

What we need to learn

  1. Did you fight up close? The whole build is an argument that close range is now the better way to play. If you still won by kiting at max range, that argument lost and we want to know.
  2. Did you notice the gauge? Under your health, filling as you fight. Did you work out what it did before it went off?
  3. Did you use the kegs on purpose, or did they just go off near you? Did one ever kill you?
  4. Can you see what is shot at you? If something hit you that you never saw, that is still the bug we most want.
  5. Where did you die, and did it feel fair? Note the wave. Being overwhelmed should feel like a crowd problem, not a numbers one.
  6. Frame rate. Hit F3 during a busy wave and tell us the number and your machine.
