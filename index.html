SKYGEAR

Hero defense · 10,000 feet · playtest build

You are a sky-pirate captain defending the deck of your airship. Every skill is a shape crossed with an element — 32 combinations — and the draft after each wave rewrites them. Twelve waves. Keep the Boiler alive.

v10 · For a stranger

The first build made for someone who has never seen it: it starts instantly instead of waiting on 24 MB of art, you choose your opening weapon instead of being handed one, the run ends on a results screen you can read, and everything is reachable from a settings menu. Same twelve waves, same Boiler, far less to explain.

Score & passives

v9 · Score & passives

Music — a brass-and-storm theme, looped from its sustained middle with a crossfade so it never drops to silence. And passive skills: a Field that burns everything near you, a Pulse that detonates on its own, a Sentry you plant and forget. They take a slot and cross with all four elements like anything else, so a four-skill build no longer has to mean four buttons to press.

Builds

v8 · Builds

A playtester finished all twelve waves using exactly one ability, because every upgrade card funnelled into it and taking a second skill halved that. Now a slot unlocking gives you a draft of three weapons — you pick which, not whether — and new element-wide cards mean committing to Frost across four shapes is a build rather than a dilution.

Sound

v7 · Sound

The audio pass. Real recorded cues layered over the synth that has always been there — and six sounds that were plain wrong are fixed: your own deck cannon no longer fires the enemy gunshot, reinforcements no longer arrive on a UI blip, and the boarding hulk taking damage, your crew dying and a turret being worn down are all audible for the first time. Turn it up.

Real basic attack

v6 · Real basic

Your auto-attack is the Ember Cleave now — the actual 140° sweep, element and crit, swung on its own at whatever you're facing, not a weaker generic flick beside it. Both mouse buttons are free for abilities. A second cargo-run passage opens up forward, so you can rotate between lanes at the front line instead of walking the deck. First build with the painted art on by default.

v5 · Lanes

The MOBA restructure. Three hard-separated lanes, a deck cannon gating each one, your own crew pushing up them, and the Boiler moved to the stern like a nexus. Two waves are pushes: their boarding hulk opens and you have to leave the objective to break it.

What we need to learn

  1. Does the build-craft loop land? Did you ever look forward to the draft, or did you just take whatever was cheapest?
  2. Where did you die, and did it feel fair? Note the wave number. Getting overwhelmed should feel like a crowd problem, not a numbers problem.
  3. Can you read the fight? Every enemy attack telegraphs for 0.4s before it lands. If something hit you that you never saw, that is the bug we most want.
  4. Storm-Dusk vs Classic: is anything harder to track in the three-quarter view — your own character, enemy facing, where a mortar will land?
  5. Frame rate. Hit F3 during a busy wave and tell us the number and your machine. This is the one thing we have not been able to measure properly.
